Privacy Policy

Mic Check — Can U Hear Me Now

Short version: your audio never leaves your device.

Mic Check helps you confirm your microphone is producing audio before you speak in a Microsoft Teams meeting. To do that it reads your microphone's input level using your browser's Web Audio API.

What we process

  • Live microphone level (loudness), computed in-memory only while a check is running.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not record, store, or buffer your audio to disk.
  • We do not transmit audio, transcripts, or level data to any server.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking, cookies, or third-party data sharing.

Permissions

The app requests microphone access solely to measure input level. Access is released the moment you stop a check or close the panel.

This is an open-source project (MIT). Questions or concerns? Open an issue at github.com/rod-trent/mic-check.
